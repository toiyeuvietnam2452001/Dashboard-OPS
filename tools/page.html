          Risk assessment by root cause—

          Each root cause scored 1–5 in the ORR Check list file, with the average score, risk status and action plan as written there.

          ItemActivityResourceInterfaceScopeVendorContractAvg. scoreRisk statusAction plan
          How the risk is scored (Root cause sheet)
          No.Root CauseDescription
          1Resource Constraints- Insufficient manpower or resources not allocated at the required time.
          - Applications/ systems have not been ready yet.
          2Interface Issues- Pending information, data, documents, or approvals from other departments, Vendors/ contractors, or stakeholders.
          - Preceding activities have not been completed, preventing the subsequent activities from proceeding as planned.
          3Scope Changes- Changes to design, scope of work, technical requirements, or Client/Partner requirements.
          - Additional or unforeseen work has been identified, requiring the planned activities or documents to be revised or reworked.
          4Vendor / Contractor IssuesVendor/Contractor has not met the agreed schedule, deliverable requirements, mobilization plan, or other contractual commitments, resulting in delays to the planned activity.
          5Contract / Budget / ApprovalBudget, contract, procurement, commercial arrangements, or required approvals/decisions have not been finalized within the planned time frame, preventing the activity from proceeding as scheduled.
          ScoreMức độÝ nghĩa
          1NegligibleKhông có trở ngại đáng kể; hoàn toàn trong kiểm soát
          2LowCó vấn đề nhỏ nhưng đã kiểm soát; không ảnh hưởng target date
          3ModerateCó rủi ro thực sự; cần action/mitigation để bảo đảm tiến độ
          4HighRủi ro đáng kể; khả năng trễ cao nếu không có intervention/recovery
          5Critical / BlockedĐang bị blocked hoặc không có credible path để hoàn thành đúng hạn
          StatusĐiều kiện
          REDCó root cause = 5, hoặc có từ 3 root cause = 4 trở lên
          YELLOWKhông thỏa RED và có ít nhất một root cause = 3 hoặc 4
          GREENCả 5 root cause ≤ 2

          Average Risk Score = trung bình 5 điểm, từ 1,0 đến 5,0; dùng để xếp ưu tiên trong cùng một màu. Action Plan: hướng giải quyết cho các root cause ≥ 3.
